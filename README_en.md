Created by **Antre des Loutres**, **Cobblemon RLM** expands [Cobblemon](https://cobblemon.com) with Pokémon from our universe, along with exclusive abilities and moves.

> Since version **1.7.0**, the datapack and its resources are bundled in a **mod**. Install the Fabric or NeoForge `.jar` to use its content and registered Mega Gem items.

## The things added by this addon

<details>
  <summary>Explore the RLM regional forms</summary>

### RLM Meganium — Grass / Steel

A fierce guardian who reserves its loyalty for Trainers who earn its respect. Its family includes **RLM Chikorita** and **Bayleef**.

Its **Verdant Bastion** ability punishes contact attacks and sets Spikes. Its **Florasteel Charge** move also sets Spikes while attacking.

### RLM Blastoise — Water / Flying

Its cannons act as thrusters that launch it into the air. **RLM Squirtle** and **Wartortle** already practise riding rising air currents.

Its **Marine Vortex** ability summons rain and boosts its offensive stats. **High Propulsion** gives it a priority Flying-type attack.

### RLM Empoleon — Water / Electric

Usually calm, it unleashes torrents of electricity when angered. Its family includes **RLM Piplup** and **Prinplup**.

### RLM Delphox — Fire / Fairy

A mischievous forest magician who plays tricks on travellers while secretly guiding those who get lost. **RLM Fennekin** and **Braixen** share its affinity for magic.

Its **Psycho-Prio** ability increases move priority while it has more than half its HP. **Mystic Exchange** turns stat changes to its advantage.

### RLM Ampharos — Electric / Ghost

The orb on its tail guides lost souls to the afterlife. **RLM Mareep** and **Flaaffy** also carry spectral energy in their wool.

### RLM Probopass — Rock / Fairy

A familiar sight on the beach, it enjoys relaxing alongside humans. **RLM Nosepass** shares its laid-back nature and dual typing.

### RLM Spiritomb — Fire / Dark

Wearing an Oni mask and inhabiting a Jizo statue, it wards off evil spirits. Its **Soul Release** ability retaliates based on the number of fainted allies.

### RLM Luxray — Ice

From snowy peaks, it watches over its pack of **RLM Shinx** and **Luxio**. Its **Frosty Slide** move strikes with +2 priority.

### RLM Togekiss — Fairy / Dragon

Scarred by human conflict, it struggles to contain a draconic rage. **RLM Togepi** and **Togetic** also draw on this ancient energy.

Its **Dragonate** ability turns Normal-type moves into Dragon-type moves and boosts their power.

### RLM Nincada, Ninjask and Shedinja

- **Nincada — Bug / Water**: lives along riverbanks and can move across the water's surface.
- **Ninjask — Water / Flying**: crosses lakes at incredible speed with its webbed legs.
- **Shedinja — Water / Rock**: its shell merges with underwater coral and fossils. **Dullness Guard** protects it from neutral and super-effective attacks.

### RLM Mawile — Poison / Fairy or Dragon / Fairy

Two variants descended from **Smowile**: the **Poison** form channels a toxic aura, while the **Dragon** form develops a jaw capable of devastating attacks. Each has its own Mega Evolution.

### RLM Sunflora — Three forms

- **Sky — Grass / Flying**: claims to have been Rayquaza's teacher.
- **Ocean — Grass / Water**: insists it defeated Kyogre in a duel.
- **Volcano — Grass / Fire**: describes itself as Groudon's best friend.

Their stories are as impressive as they are difficult to verify!

### RLM Haxorus — Dragon / Poison

Its blades are infused with deadly poison. Its family includes **RLM Axew** and **Fraxure**, both **Dragon / Normal**.

Its **Venom Edge** ability boosts slicing moves and can poison their targets. **Dragon Axe** deals damage at the cost of lowering the user's Defense.

## Installation

The current project version targets **Minecraft 1.21.1**, **Java 21** and **Cobblemon 1.8.2**.

| Dependency | Required? |
|------------|-----------|
| [Cobblemon](https://modrinth.com/mod/cobblemon) and its dependencies | Yes |
| Fabric or NeoForge | Yes, matching your RLM file |
| [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api) | Yes on Fabric |
| [Cobblemon: Mega Showdown](https://modrinth.com/mod/cobblemon-mega-showdown) and its dependencies | For Mega Evolutions and Mega Gem recipes |

Development uses Fabric Loader **0.19.5**, NeoForge **21.1.251** and Mega Showdown **1.3.0+1.8.2+1.21.1-hotfix**.

<details>
  <summary>Install with Modrinth App</summary>

1. Create or open a **Minecraft 1.21.1** instance with **Fabric** or **NeoForge**.
2. Add **Cobblemon 1.8.2** and its dependencies, plus **Fabric API** if you use Fabric.
3. From the [Cobblemon RLM page](https://modrinth.com/project/cobblemon-rlm), select the file for your loader and add it to that instance.
4. Add **Mega Showdown** and its dependencies if you want to use Mega Evolutions.
5. Launch the game and open your world.

</details>

<details>
  <summary>Install manually in singleplayer or on a server</summary>

1. Install **Fabric** or **NeoForge** for Minecraft **1.21.1**, then **Cobblemon 1.8.2** and its dependencies.
2. Download the **Cobblemon RLM** `.jar` for your loader from [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/project/cobblemon-rlm).
3. Put the file in your Minecraft instance's **`mods/`** folder.
4. For multiplayer, also install the mod and its dependencies in the server's **`mods/`** folder. Client and server versions must be compatible.
5. Install **Mega Showdown** to use Mega Evolutions and craft Mega Gems.
6. Restart the game or server.

The mod includes its data, models and textures. No additional resource pack is needed.

</details>

<details>
  <summary>Update from the old datapack</summary>

Remove the old RLM ZIP from the world's **`datapacks/`** folder, any separate RLM resource pack and the old RLM JAR from **`mods/`**, then install the new mod.

The new Mega Gem items require the mod. Putting its file in `datapacks/` or `resourcepacks/` does not replace installing it in `mods/`.

Legacy dye-based gems retain their models and data. New recipes produce the registered RLM items; existing inventories are not converted automatically.

</details>

## Items, recipes and commands

<details>
  <summary>Craft the Mega Gems</summary>

All five gems are available in the **Cobblemon RLM** creative tab. With Mega Showdown installed, they can also be crafted at a **smithing table**.

Place a **Mega Stone** (`mega_showdown:mega_stone`) in the base slot, then add the Cobblemon gems listed below in the first and third slots.

| Result | First slot | Base | Addition |
|--------|------------|------|----------|
| **Ampharosite RLM** | Electric Gem | Mega Stone | Ghost Gem |
| **Flammikite** | Fire Gem | Mega Stone | Electric Gem |
| **Mawilite RLM (Dragon)** | Dragon Gem | Mega Stone | Fairy Gem |
| **Mawilite RLM (Poison)** | Poison Gem | Mega Stone | Fairy Gem |
| **Wamekite** | Bug Gem | Mega Stone | Dragon Gem |

Without Mega Showdown, the items remain in the creative tab, but their recipes and Mega Evolution effects are disabled.

</details>

<details>
  <summary>Get Pokémon and gems through commands</summary>

These commands require cheats in singleplayer or the appropriate server permissions. Species names and aspects use their identifiers regardless of your game's language.

```mcfunction
/pokegive @s flammiko
/pokegive @s rineshell
/pokegive @s tinywone
/pokegive @s wamek
/pokegive @s smowile
/pokegive @s kotora
/pokegive @s delphox rlm
/pokegive @s mawile rlm dragon
/pokegive @s mawile rlm poison
/pokegive @s sunflora rlm ciel
/pokegive @s sunflora rlm ocean
/pokegive @s sunflora rlm volcan
```

For RLM Delphox, `rlm=true` also works. Without this aspect, `/pokegive @s delphox` gives the normal form, including when Mega Showdown is installed.

```mcfunction
/give @s rlm_megas:ampharositerlm
/give @s rlm_megas:flammikite
/give @s rlm_megas:mawiliterlmdragon
/give @s rlm_megas:mawiliterlmpoison
/give @s rlm_megas:wamekite
```

With Mega Showdown installed, gems obtained through commands or the creative tab automatically receive its required Mega Evolution components.

</details>

## Development

<details>
  <summary>Build the mod and launch a development environment</summary>

Use a **JDK 21** and point `JAVA_HOME` to it. The repository includes the Gradle wrapper.

```sh
# Linux / macOS
./gradlew build
```

```powershell
# Windows
.\gradlew.bat build
```

Both installable mods are collected in **`build/libs/`**:

- `cobblemon-rlm-fabric-1.7.0.jar`
- `cobblemon-rlm-neoforge-1.7.0.jar`

Versions are defined in [`gradle.properties`](gradle.properties). Use `-Prelease_version=1.7.1` to override the version for a release build.

| Module | Role |
|--------|------|
| [`common`](common) | Shared code, items, Mega Showdown compatibility and `assets/` and `data/` resources |
| [`fabric`](fabric) | Fabric registration, creative tab and metadata |
| [`neoforge`](neoforge) | NeoForge registration, creative tab and metadata |

Common sources are compiled into each mod. RLM adds no Architectury dependency; do not install the `common` module JAR.

To launch a development client:

```sh
./gradlew :fabric:runClient
./gradlew :neoforge:runClient
```

On Windows, replace `./gradlew` with `.\gradlew.bat`. The `:fabric:runServer` and `:neoforge:runServer` tasks are also available.

These environments load **Cobblemon**, **Mega Showdown** and **JEI 19.27.0.340** by default. Use `-PwithMegaShowdown=false` to test without Mega Showdown. Development dependencies are not embedded in the distributed JARs. JEI's version is set by `jei_version`; restart the game after changing it.

Clients use up to **4 GB of memory** and automatically open **`RLM-Test`** when that save exists in the module's `runs/client/saves/` folder. Use `-PquickPlayWorld=` to open the main menu instead.

When generated locally, `build/client-test/Cobblemon-RLM-Test-1.7.0.mrpack` can be imported into Modrinth App. It includes the Fabric mod, its dependencies, JEI and the creative test world **“RLM 1.7.0 - Test creatif”**, with all five Mega Gems and a Mega Bracelet in the hotbar.

</details>

<details>
  <summary>Launch clients from IntelliJ IDEA</summary>

1. Open the repository root and import the **Gradle** project.
2. In **Settings → Build, Execution, Deployment → Build Tools → Gradle**, select the **Gradle wrapper** and a **JDK 21** as the Gradle JVM.
3. Reload the Gradle project, then select **RLM - Client Fabric** or **RLM - Client NeoForge**.
4. Click **Run ▶** to launch the client and view logs in the **Run** window.

Shared configurations live in [`.run/`](.run). You can also launch `runClient` tasks from the Gradle tool window.

</details>

<details>
  <summary>Add a gem and follow releases</summary>

To add a gem, update the [`MegaGem` enum](common/src/main/java/fr/antredesloutres/cobblemonrlm/item/MegaGem.java), its models, textures and translations, its recipe in [`data/rlm_megas/recipe/`](common/src/main/resources/data/rlm_megas/recipe) and the [`rlm_megas:mega_gems` tag](common/src/main/resources/data/rlm_megas/tags/item/mega_gems.json).

Mega Showdown presence conditions are added to recipes when building the Fabric and NeoForge mods.

See the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md) for version changes and the [publication guide](.github/RELEASE.md) for releases.

</details>
